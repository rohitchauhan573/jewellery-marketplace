Read the hallmark before you pay

Gold jewellery sold in India is stamped under the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). The stamp takes ten seconds to read and tells you what you are really paying for.

The marks are tiny, so ask the shop to show them under a magnifier. Then match them with the purity written on your bill.

BIS mark
Shows the piece was tested at a centre recognised by the Bureau of Indian Standards.
Purity, such as 22K916
22 carat gold is 91.6% pure. The number 916 is that figure without the decimal point.
HUID
A 6-character code that belongs to this one piece. You can look it up in the BIS Care app.

What the carat number means

Carat tells you how much of the piece is pure gold. The rest is other metal that makes it hard enough to wear. These are the purities you can filter by on this page.

Looking after your jewellery

  • Keep each piece in its own soft pouch so chains do not scratch other jewellery.
  • Put jewellery on after perfume, hairspray and makeup, not before. Keep pearls away from perfume and cleaners.
  • Take it off before bathing, swimming and workouts.
  • Clean gold with mild soap, warm water and a soft brush, then dry it fully.
  • Once a year, ask a jeweller to check the clasps and stone settings.

Before you pay

  • Ask for a bill that lists weight, purity and making charges on separate lines.
  • Check that the hallmark and HUID on the piece match the bill.
  • For stone jewellery, ask for the stone weight separately. Stones are not priced as gold.
  • Ask about exchange and buyback rules before you pay.
  • Compare the price with the gold rate shown at the top of this page. The fair price check on each product does this for you.

Common questions

How does the fair price check work?

We take the gold rate set for the day and multiply it by the piece's weight and purity. Then we add making charges and an estimated tax, and compare the total with the shop's price. It is an estimate: stones and detailed design work can change a real price.

How accurate is the virtual try-on?

It places the piece using points on your face, so the fit is approximate. Your camera image is processed in your browser and is not uploaded.

Is the payment real?

No. Jewellery Wala is a project demo. The advance and online payment options are practice only, and no money moves.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, while it is still pending. Open My orders and choose Cancel order. The stock goes back to the shop.

What is the delivery code for?

Every order has a 4-digit code. Give it to the delivery person only when the jewellery is in your hands. It confirms the delivery.

How can my shop sell here?

Register as a shop owner and submit your shop details. After the admin approves it, you can add pieces with photos and manage your own stock.
